Delegations

Plan a Purposeful Business Delegation to Ghana

A well-designed delegation combines clear objectives, suitable participants, relevant meetings, practical logistics and disciplined follow-up. The Private Office coordinates each stage through one accountable programme.

Plan a Business Delegation
Suitable delegations

Designed around legitimate objectives

Suitable programmes include trade associations, chambers of commerce, corporate leadership teams, investors, professional bodies, universities, diaspora business groups and public-private missions with a lawful commercial or institutional purpose.

01

Before the visit

We hold a planning consultation, define the desired outcomes, collect participant information, research counterparties, design the programme and submit appropriate meeting requests.

02

During the visit

The approved programme may include business and institutional meetings, company visits, factory or farm visits, project and site visits, round-table discussions, executive briefings, interpreters and coordinated transport.

03

After the visit

We prepare an action summary, record agreed next steps and, where commissioned, coordinate follow-up with participating organisations.

Lead time

Allow time for preparation

Submit enquiries as early as possible. Senior institutional meetings and specialised site visits require sufficient preparation and remain subject to the independent availability and approval of the host organisation.

What to provide

Information for a useful programme

Organisation profile; delegation leader; participant number and seniority; preferred dates; sectors; target organisations; specific objectives; language needs; accessibility needs; and desired outputs.

Important note

The Private Office does not issue visas or guarantee visas, meeting confirmations or official access. Travellers remain responsible for passports, visas, insurance, health requirements and personal compliance.
