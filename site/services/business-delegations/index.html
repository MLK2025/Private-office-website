Service 03

Business Delegations and Executive Visits

Purposeful Ghana programmes designed around business objectives, not ceremonial activity.

Plan a Business Delegation
01

Who this is for

Companies, chambers, associations, investors, project teams, universities and senior executives planning a structured visit to Ghana.

02

The problem it solves

A valuable visit requires the right meetings, realistic travel time, reliable logistics, careful briefing and disciplined follow-up. We combine these elements within one programme.

03

What the service may include

Pre-visit consultation; objective setting; participant information; programme and itinerary design; business and institutional meeting requests; company, factory, farm and project visits; executive briefings; airport reception coordination; hotels, transport and interpreters through approved suppliers; meeting materials; and post-visit follow-up.

04

Programme approval

Proposed meetings remain subject to the availability and independent agreement of each organisation. No meeting with a public official, company or institution is represented as confirmed until written confirmation has been received.

05

How the assignment begins

Submit the delegation objective, preferred dates, number and seniority of participants, sectors of interest and desired outcomes. Allow adequate lead time for research and meeting requests.

06

After the visit

We issue an action summary and, where commissioned, coordinate approved correspondence and next steps.
