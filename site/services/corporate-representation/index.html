Service 02

Corporate Representation in Ghana

A reliable local point of coordination for approved commercial activity.

Request Corporate Representation
01

Who this is for

International companies and organisations that require a continuing Ghanaian presence before establishing a full local office or appointing a permanent team.

02

The problem it solves

Distance can slow communication, weaken follow-up and leave opportunities without a responsible local owner. We provide agreed representation within a clear mandate.

03

What the service may include

Local correspondence; meeting arrangements; opportunity monitoring; stakeholder and partner follow-up; preparation of local activity notes; coordination with appointed advisers; executive-visit support; and periodic written reports.

04

Mandate and limits

The representation agreement states what we may communicate, arrange and report. We do not sign contracts, make binding commitments, collect funds, provide regulated advice or act outside written authority.

05

Reporting

Clients receive an agreed schedule of activity reports recording communications, meetings, decisions required, open matters and recommended next actions.

06

How the assignment begins

Provide the organisation’s profile, Ghana objective, intended stakeholders, expected duration and authority required. Following due diligence, we propose a retainer or defined project mandate.
