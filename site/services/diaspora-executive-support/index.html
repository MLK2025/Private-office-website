Service 07

Diaspora and International Executive Support

Coordinated local support for serious business, investment and relocation visits.

Request Executive Support
01

Who this is for

Diaspora professionals, international executives and families arranging a Ghanaian business, investment, property or relocation programme.

02

The problem it solves

Clients may need several appointments and service providers but lack a trusted point of coordination. We organise the programme and appropriate professional referrals.

03

What the service may include

Business appointments; investment and site visits; property-viewing schedules with licensed professionals; transport and accommodation coordination; local orientation; administrative appointment support; and introductions to qualified lawyers, accountants, immigration practitioners, banks, insurers and property professionals.

04

Professional advice

The Private Office coordinates. Each regulated professional provides independent advice under a separate engagement and remains responsible for that advice.

05

How the assignment begins

Submit the purpose of the visit, preferred period, participants, required appointments, location and any professional services needed.

06

Privacy

Only information necessary to coordinate the approved assignment should be collected. Sensitive personal documents should be transferred through an agreed secure channel, not ordinary website forms.
