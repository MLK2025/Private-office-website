Service 05

Institutional Partnership Services

Careful preparation and coordination for legitimate institutional cooperation.

Discuss an Institutional Partnership
01

Who this is for

Associations, foundations, universities, traditional institutions, municipalities, development organisations and companies seeking an appropriate Ghanaian institutional relationship.

02

The problem it solves

Institutional approaches often fail when the purpose is vague, the wrong department is contacted or the proposal offers no practical value. We help define and route a credible proposition.

03

What the service may include

Institutional mapping; contact and mandate research; concept notes; meeting requests; agenda and briefing preparation; coordination of visits; minutes; action tracking; and support towards a suitable cooperation document.

04

Appropriate instruments

Depending on the relationship, the proper outcome may be an exchange of letters, referral arrangement, collaboration memorandum, supplier agreement or project-specific contract. An MOU is not required for every relationship.

05

How the assignment begins

Provide the intended institution, cooperation objective, proposed contribution, expected public benefit, timetable and any existing relationship.

06

Independence

The Office cannot commit an institution, public authority or third party. All decisions remain with the relevant organisation and authorised officeholders.
