Service 08

Project and Partnership Origination

Early-stage coordination for credible projects and commercial opportunities.

Submit a Project Enquiry
Defined scopeAccountable coordinationDocumented follow-through
01

Who this is for

Project sponsors, investors, technical companies, landowners, operating businesses and Dadaba Group companies considering suitable opportunities.

02

The problem it solves

Promising ideas often lack a clear sponsor, verified information, capable partners or an agreed route to development. We help organise the early relationship and information flow.

03

What the service may include

Initial opportunity screening; sponsor and stakeholder identification; preliminary information memorandum; partner and investor research; meeting coordination; early action plan; and referral to qualified legal, financial, environmental and technical advisers.

04

Screening criteria

We consider the sponsor, ownership, approvals, commercial logic, information quality, capital requirement, environmental and social context, delivery capacity and potential conflicts.

05

Relationship with Dadaba Group

Where appropriate, an opportunity may be referred to the relevant Dadaba Group company. Any further participation is subject to a separate decision, due diligence, agreement and regulatory responsibility.

06

How the assignment begins

Submit a concise project summary, sponsor details, location, current stage, ownership or control evidence, approvals status, capital requirement and the partnership sought.

Work in context

Local understanding, visible engagement

The assignment is grounded in real places, capable organisations and direct professional engagement. Research and introductions are always shaped around the client's stated objective.

Working sequence

From a clear brief to an accountable next step

01

Clarify

Define the objective, decision owner, essential criteria and realistic timing.

02

Map

Research the landscape, relevant stakeholders and information that must be verified.

03

Coordinate

Arrange the approved engagement, briefing, visit or local follow-through.

04

Record

Document findings, decisions, open questions and responsible next actions.
