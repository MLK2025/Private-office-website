Service 04

Trade and Partnership Matchmaking

Structured connections between credible organisations with a defined commercial purpose.

Submit a Partnership Enquiry
01

Who this is for

Foreign buyers, Ghanaian suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, investors, project sponsors, associations and companies seeking capable international or local partners.

02

The problem it solves

A contact list is not a partnership. We begin with criteria, examine potential fit and coordinate a responsible first discussion.

03

What the service may include

Partner criteria; market and company research; longlisting; initial qualification; conflict and reputation checks using lawful sources; meeting coordination; briefing notes; exchange of approved information; and follow-up.

04

Qualification principles

Potential counterparties are assessed against the agreed purpose, apparent capacity, location, sector relevance and available public information. Full legal, financial, technical and regulatory due diligence remains the responsibility of qualified advisers and the contracting parties.

05

How the assignment begins

Submit the product, opportunity or partnership sought; target geography; capacity requirements; commercial model; timetable; and essential qualification criteria.

06

No success guarantee

We do not guarantee that an introduction will result in a contract, investment, distributorship or official support.
